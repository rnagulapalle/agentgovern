## Outcome

Help people with a concrete workflow problem discover relevant LoopLabs proof and book a founder call. Optimize for qualified booked and attended calls, then accepted bounded pilots. Articles, impressions, followers and CTA clicks are supporting signals, not the goal.

Qualification needs observed evidence: workflow ownership or access to its owner; a recurring consequential action or manual handoff; a specific loss, delay, uncertainty or approval problem; the current workaround; and willingness to explore one bounded pilot. Do not infer a budget, urgency or fit from a job title, comment or anonymous visit. Keep unknown fields unknown.

Measure separately: relevant discovery, proof engagement, accepted sales request, confirmed booking, attended qualified conversation, agreed pilot. Count booking only from an actual calendar reservation or authorized booking record, not a button click. No reliable confirmed/qualified-call baseline has been collected yet. Private contact details and calendar records remain outside public Git; repository reports contain aggregates or anonymized references.

## Recovery contract

At each executor/watchdog/follow-through run compare the last seven days of expected Pacific-time slots against journal rows and action evidence. Missing rows mean unverified completion. Inspect commits, files and existing public destinations before retrying. Complete one oldest still-relevant, incomplete, authorized step; record the original slot, actual recovery time and final evidence. Keep historical failed/missing records intact. Never backdate success.

Use stable action IDs in the queue. Before acting, claim the due action with an actual attempt timestamp; other runs must not take the same in-progress action. If a stale in-progress action exists, inspect its side effects before reclaiming it. Cross-process claims require an exclusive local lock when runs overlap; inability to establish exclusive ownership means defer the external mutation. Retry only incomplete portions; never repeat posts, uploads, accepted intake, indexing requests or deployments blindly. Record uncertain outcomes and inspect before retry.

Canceled or outdated work is explicitly marked superseded/canceled with evidence, not executed to fill a journal gap. October 3's unverified nightly completion remains historical; the current weekly evidence/proof package supersedes its stale content objective. Product Hunt launch stays canceled.

Safe API/repository work can recover without the browser. Browser-dependent work remains queued until the authorized session is available. After two blocked recovery attempts or an overdue due date, report the exact blocker and one required user action. Preserve privacy, permission boundaries and existing release gates. A cloud-independent scheduler/executor has not been built: these instructions improve recovery when Codex runs; they cannot run while the host/app is unavailable.
