Microsoft's Copilot guidance got clearer: prompts and responses can be stored in users' Exchange mailboxes — and searched via eDiscovery when your tenant is configured for it.

For compliance leads at mid-size companies, that's the retention piece you've been asking about.

But retention is not governance.

Mailbox logs tell you what Copilot **said**. They don't tell you whether Copilot **sent** an email, **changed** a CRM record, or **exported** a patient list — unless you built controls for those actions separately.

Before you scale Copilot (or ChatGPT Enterprise) across a 200–800 person org, verify four things:

→ Your SKU actually supports the eDiscovery workflow you think you have  
→ You've run a **test search** for Copilot interactions — not just read the admin doc  
→ SharePoint permissions are cleaned up (Copilot inherits access)  
→ You have **approval rules** for external email, record changes, and exports
